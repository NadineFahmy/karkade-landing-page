We started with one pot and a stubborn recipe.

In 2021, two friends in Giza tried to make the karkade they remembered from childhood. It took 40 batches to get it right.

Today we work with three family farms in Aswan, pay them above market price, and brew every bottle ourselves. No powders, no syrups, no shortcuts.
